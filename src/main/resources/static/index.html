📍 What are QR Field Markers?
Print these QR codes and attach them to physical stakes in each plot. When scanned with any smartphone camera, they show:
  • 🌾 Crop history for that specific plot
  • 🌱 Current soil health status
  • 📅 Upcoming harvest dates
  • 💧 Water usage & recommendations
